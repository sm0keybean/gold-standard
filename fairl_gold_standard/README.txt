Gold Standard is a display-only currency studio. Presets provide editable labels, not historical exchange rates. Native construction and floating numeric conversion is NOT implemented; raw game-unit labels are the default. This is an unverified in-game preview, not an officially curated release.

USING OTHER CURRENCIES
Japanese yen is included as a preset. For Korean won, Russian ruble or another real or fictional currency, choose Create your own and enter a 1–12-character label: for example ¥ / JPY, ₩ / KRW, or ₽ / RUB.
Set the conversion rate, base year and annual display inflation/deflation. Choose decimal places (0 for whole units), grouping separators and label position. Select Apply to this save, then save your game to retain the settings.
Rates are user-defined, not live exchange rates or verified historical conversions. Symbol rendering depends on the game's font; use JPY, KRW or RUB if a symbol is unavailable.
